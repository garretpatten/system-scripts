Personal Calendar Tasks Brief

Personal Calendar Tasks Brief is a personal, single-user tool that generates a morning brief for its owner. It reads the owner's Google Calendar events and Google Tasks for the day and produces a short summary of what is scheduled and what needs to be done.

This app is for personal use only. It is not offered to other users, has no accounts, and does not collect data from anyone else.

Read the Privacy Policy for details on what Google user data this app accesses and how it is handled.
